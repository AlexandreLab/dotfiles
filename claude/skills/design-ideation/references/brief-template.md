# Brief template

Fill every section. Each is 1–4 lines. Whole file stays under ~60 lines. Use the headings exactly.

```markdown
# <Direction name>

**Subject:** <what is being designed, for whom>
**Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>

## 1. Concept
One paragraph. The world, material, or metaphor this design lives in, and why it fits the subject.

## 2. Mood
Five adjectives.

## 3. Colour
Palette with hex codes and roles (background, surface, ink, accent, signal). Note contrast intent.

## 4. Typography
Display and body families (real, available fonts or close fallbacks), scale, one quirk.

## 5. Layout and composition
Grid or anti-grid, density, asymmetry, negative space, how sections flow.

## 6. Motion and interaction
What moves, how fast, what it communicates. Include what does NOT move.

## 7. Signature details
Three specific touches that make this unmistakable.

## 8. Rule broken on purpose
Which convention this ignores, and why it still works here.

## 9. Avoid
Clichés and AI tells that would cheapen this specific direction.

## 10. Build prompt
Under 150 words, self-contained, paste-ready for a coding agent. States subject, direction,
palette, type, layout, motion, and the one thing it must nail. No references to this file.
```
